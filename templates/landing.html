How TrustHarvest Works
01
Farmer Registers Batch
Name, quantity, farming method, packaging date and photo are recorded. A unique Batch ID and QR code are generated.
02
Hash Stored On-Chain
A SHA-256 fingerprint of batch data is computed and written to the Ethereum smart contract via Web3.py.
03
Supply Chain Updates
Transporters log checkpoints and retailers confirm stock. Status is updated in the database while hashes remain immutable on-chain.
04
Consumer Scans & Verifies
QR code opens the verify page. Database hash is recomputed and compared against the contract — showing Verified or Tampered.
