How Cryptographic Tamper Detection Works
SHA-256 + Ethereum Smart Contract
1 The Digital Seal (Origin)

When produce is logged at the farm, core fields (Origin, Quantity, Method, Date, Batch ID) are passed into SHA-256 to generate an unalterable 64-character fingerprint.

2 The Immutable Ledger

This hash is stored on the Ethereum blockchain. Unlike traditional databases, blockchain transactions cannot be edited, deleted, or overwritten by any administrator.

3 Zero-Trust Fraud Detection

When you verify, TrustHarvest recalculates the hash from the current database. If even a single comma was altered, the hash changes completely — instantly exposing tampering!
